# Staffing And Budget

## Baseline Team (Recommended)

- 1 Technical Lead (Unity/.NET integration)
- 2 Unity Gameplay/Client Engineers
- 1 Backend Engineer (host/network/reconnect)
- 1 Technical Artist / 3D Generalist
- 1 UI/UX Designer
- 1 QA Engineer (plus external test support during beta)

## Optional Roles

- Audio designer (part-time)
- Producer / Project manager
- DevOps support

## Budget Drivers

- team size and seniority
- outsourced 3D asset volume
- QA depth and device/network matrix
- live operations support scope

## ROM Cost Envelope (Order Of Magnitude)

- Lean team, 10-12 months: medium six figures (USD)
- Full production polish, 12-18 months: high six to low seven figures (USD)

## Capacity Risks

- single-point failure on architecture decisions
- underestimating animation/content workload
- insufficient multiplayer QA in unstable networks

## Burn Control Recommendations

- freeze core scope at vertical slice signoff
- gate polish work behind playability/stability goals
- track weekly throughput and defect trends
